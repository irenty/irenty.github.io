run-sync privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

run-sync is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Strava data.

What it accesses

run-sync requests read-only Strava access (activity:read_all). It uses it to read the owner's own activities and their recorded data, such as distance, pace, heart rate and elevation. It never creates, modifies or deletes anything in the Strava account, and it reads no other athlete's data.

Where data is stored

Data is stored only on machines the owner controls, in a local database. The OAuth tokens are stored the same way and are never published.

Sharing

Strava data is not sold or shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. Excerpts of the owner's own training data may be sent to an AI model provider to produce personal training feedback for the owner. It is not used to train AI models.

Retention and revoking access

The owner can delete the local data at any time. Access can be revoked at any time in Strava under Settings → My Apps.
