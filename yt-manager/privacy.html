yt-manager privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

yt-manager is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What it accesses

yt-manager requests the read-only YouTube scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly). It uses it to read the owner's subscriptions, playlists and the videos in them. It never modifies the YouTube account.

Where data is stored

Data is stored only on machines the owner controls: a local database and local files. The OAuth token is stored the same way and is never published.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. The captions of public videos may be sent to an AI model provider to produce personal summaries for the owner; subscription and account data is not.

Retention and revoking access

The owner can delete the local data at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
